# FloorSight — dealer risk intelligence (demo)

Sales materials for FloorSight, a dealer trust-score / risk-management product for
banks that provide floorplan financing to car dealers.

- **[Pitch deck](https://fleetbitapp.github.io/floorsight/)** — keynote-style animated
  presentation (arrow keys / click to advance, F fullscreen, B blackout).
- **[Console demo](https://fleetbitapp.github.io/floorsight/console.html)** — interactive
  walkthrough of the risk console: trust score, portfolio view, dealer risk files,
  lot-check collateral map, alerts, and the deposit engine.

All dealers, figures and events are fictional and illustrative.
